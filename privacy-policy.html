HealthBot GH Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

HealthBot GH is a digital health information assistant designed to provide accessible and easy-to-understand health information through WhatsApp and web-based services in Ghana.

Information We Collect

HealthBot GH may process information that users voluntarily provide when interacting with the service, including WhatsApp messages, account details, and conversation content required to provide responses.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate HealthBot GH, respond to user queries, improve the service, maintain conversation history where applicable, and support the security and reliability of the platform.

Health Information

HealthBot GH provides general health information only and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care. Users should seek qualified medical assistance for medical concerns and emergencies.

Third-Party Services

HealthBot GH may use third-party services such as Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, cloud hosting providers, database services, and artificial intelligence providers to operate the service. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect user information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain system security.

Your Rights

Users may request information about their stored data or request deletion where applicable by contacting HealthBot GH.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact the HealthBot GH project administrator through the official HealthBot GH Facebook Page.
